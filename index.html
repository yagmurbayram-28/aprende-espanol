How to use this site

1 Work through the Textbook unit by unit — each one has a dialogue, vocabulary (with audio), detailed grammar with practice, four-skills work, a culture note and a task.

2 Review words daily with Flashcards — a spaced-repetition system remembers what you find hard.

3 Train your ears in Listening and your pronunciation in Speaking (works best in Chrome/Edge). Hover over or tap any Spanish word to see what it means.

4 When you finish, dive into the Free Resources library to keep going — everything there is 100% free.

Your progress is saved automatically in this browser.
