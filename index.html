How to use this site

1 Work through the Lessons in order — each one has vocabulary (with audio), a grammar explanation, examples, and a quiz.

2 Review words daily with Flashcards — a spaced-repetition system remembers what you find hard.

3 Train your ears in Listening and your pronunciation in Speaking (works best in Chrome/Edge).

4 When you finish, dive into the Free Resources library to keep going — everything there is 100% free.
